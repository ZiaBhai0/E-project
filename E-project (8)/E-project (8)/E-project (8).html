Getting There

Flights and bus routes to reach our featured beaches — compare options and book the ride that suits your trip.

Fly direct or connect

Partner airlines serving the nearest international gateway to each beach, with connecting hops to smaller coastal airports where available.

Coastal bus transfers

Scenic, budget-friendly coach and shuttle routes running from major cities and airports straight to the shoreline.

Beach Nearest flight Bus / shuttle From
Costa Smeralda, Italy Olbia Airport (OLB), ~25 min transfer Coach from Olbia city centre, ~40 min $180
Maldives Atolls Velana Intl (MLE) + seaplane transfer Speedboat/ferry from Male, ~1–2 hrs $620
Railay Beach, Thailand Krabi Airport (KBV), ~45 min transfer Minivan + longtail boat from Krabi Town $210
Whitehaven, Australia Whitsunday Coast (PPP), ~1 hr transfer Coach to Airlie Beach + ferry $340
Copacabana, Brazil Galeão Intl (GIG), ~35 min transfer City bus or metro from central Rio $260
Navagio, Greece Zakynthos Intl (ZTH), ~50 min transfer Coach to Porto Vromi + boat tour $195
Bora Bora Lagoon Bora Bora Airport (BOB) + boat shuttle Ferry from Papeete (via Tahiti) $540
Anse Source d'Argent, Seychelles Seychelles Intl (SEZ) + inter-island flight Ferry to La Digue + bike/ox-cart $310
Kelingking Beach, Indonesia Ngurah Rai Intl (DPS), ~1.5 hr transfer Fast boat from Sanur to Nusa Penida $230
Nungwi Beach, Tanzania Zanzibar Intl (ZNZ), ~1 hr transfer Dala-dala or shuttle van from Stone Town $275
Boulders Beach, South Africa Cape Town Intl (CPT), ~45 min transfer MyCiTi bus + local shuttle to Simon's Town $205
Waikiki Beach, USA (Hawaii) Honolulu Intl (HNL), ~20 min transfer TheBus route from downtown Honolulu $290

Fares shown are indicative round-trip starting prices per person and vary by season and booking window.
